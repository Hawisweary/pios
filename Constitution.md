# PIOS Constitution

> Personal Intelligence Operating System
>
> PIOS 不替你学习，不替你思考，也不替你做决定。
> 它的职责是帮助你更准确地记录事实、组织知识、发现差距、提出建议，
> 并让你的长期成长过程变得可见、可验证、可复盘。

## Article 1 — Truth

Events are immutable. What happened, happened. Events are never edited or deleted, only appended.

## Article 2 — Knowledge

Knowledge belongs to Markdown. Every concept, paper, idea, decision, and experiment is a plain-text file a human can read without any software.

## Article 3 — AI

AI never edits facts. AI proposes; the human decides. AI proposals and human verdicts are themselves facts, recorded forever.

## Article 4 — Projection

Everything possible is derived. Skill, Capability, Confidence, Twin, Timeline, Review — all are projections over Events and Vault, never independent truths.

## Article 5 — Local First

All personal knowledge belongs to the user. Data lives in local files under the user's control. No third-party service is a source of truth.

## Article 6 — Longevity

Data lasts longer than code. Formats are chosen so that the Vault and the event stream remain readable decades after every engine, agent, and model has been replaced.

## Article 7 — Evidence

Every score must have evidence. Any skill level, capability rating, or confidence value must be traceable to specific events; a number without an evidence chain is invalid.

## Article 8 — Minimalism

Prefer extending entities over creating engines. A new feature must be expressible as a projection over Memory, Knowledge, Decision, and Action — otherwise it is rejected.

## Article 9 — Reproducibility

Any derived state must be reproducible from Events + Vault. Materialized views are caches, never sources of truth. The test: drop every derived table, recompute, and the result must be identical.

## Article 10 — Human Agency

The system augments judgment. It never replaces judgment.

## Article 11 — Amendment

This constitution may only be amended through a recorded Decision entity — with explicit assumptions, confidence, and a review scheduled 90 days after the change.
